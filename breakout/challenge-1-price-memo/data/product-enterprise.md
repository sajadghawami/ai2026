# PlanPilot Enterprise (Alvora), planned

- Status: launch planned for next quarter. No price set yet.
- Features: everything in Pro, plus SSO, audit log, portfolio view and dedicated support.
- Target: companies with more than 100 users. Alvora has 35 such customers on Pro today.
- Cost of dedicated support: about €6 per user per month.
- In a survey of those 35 customers, 22 said they "would consider" an upgrade. 14 of them named SSO as the main reason.
- Alvora's CEO wants the Enterprise plan to be at least 40% more expensive than Pro (Pro is €29 per user per month).
