# Tender: review of the tariff structure

**Client:** Stadtwerke Lindental, a regional electricity and gas supplier with 180,000 household customers.

## Background

Our tariffs have grown over fifteen years. We have 23 electricity tariffs and 11 gas tariffs. Customers don't understand them, and our margin per customer has fallen for three years.

## What we ask for

1. An analysis of the current tariff portfolio and its profitability.
2. A proposal for a simplified tariff structure with no more than 8 electricity and 5 gas tariffs.
3. An estimate of the effect on margin and on customer churn.
4. A plan for moving existing customers to the new tariffs.

## Conditions

- The project must finish within 12 weeks of the start.
- The start must be no later than 1 December 2026. We plan to sign the contract on 2 November 2026.
- Budget ceiling: €240,000 excluding VAT. Offers above it are excluded.
- At least one reference from the energy sector within the last three years is mandatory.
- The project lead must be on site in Lindental at least two days per week.

## Format of the proposal

At most two pages, in this order: understanding of the task, approach, team, timeline, fee, references.

## Evaluation

Approach 40%, fee 30%, team and references 30%.

**Deadline for proposals:** 16 October 2026, 12:00.
