# Tender: dealer pricing and discounts

**Client:** Holzwerk Brandt, a furniture manufacturer selling through 340 independent dealers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

## Background

Every dealer has an individually negotiated discount. Nobody can say why a dealer gets 28% and another one 41%. Our sales team spends the last quarter of each year renegotiating.

## What we ask for

1. An analysis of the current dealer discounts and what explains them.
2. A new discount system based on clear criteria.
3. A simulation of winners and losers among the dealers.
4. Training for the 14 members of our sales team.

## Conditions

- Results for points 1 to 3 are needed before 28 February 2027.
- Budget ceiling: €150,000 excluding VAT.
- A reference in a business that sells through dealers is required.
- The proposal must name the people who will work on the project and their roles.

## Format of the proposal

At most one and a half pages: approach, team, timeline, fee, references.

## Evaluation

Fee 40%, approach 35%, references 25%.

**Deadline for proposals:** 23 October 2026.
