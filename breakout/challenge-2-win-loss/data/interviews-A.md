# Interview notes, case A

**Interview 1 (lost, 120 trucks).** Buyer chose RouteOne. Said our quote was about 15% higher and "the tools looked the same in the demo". Liked our support team. Decision was made by procurement, not by the dispatch manager who wanted us.

**Interview 2 (won, 45 trucks).** Switched from spreadsheets. Main reason: the pilot cut empty kilometres by 8% in four weeks. Price "was never the topic once we saw the pilot numbers". Would have liked monthly billing.

**Interview 3 (lost, 300 trucks).** Stayed with their current system. "We could not see the savings for a fleet our size; your case studies are all from small fleets." Price was not discussed in detail. IT was worried about the integration effort.

**Interview 4 (won, 80 trucks).** Compared us with RouteOne. We were more expensive, but "your driver app was the only one our drivers accepted". Asked for and got a 7% discount for a three-year contract.

**Interview 5 (lost, 60 trucks).** Budget was cut in the middle of the process. "Nothing to do with you. Call us again in spring." Liked the product and the price per truck model.

**Interview 6 (lost, 150 trucks).** Chose FleetPath. Objected to our pricing per truck: "In winter a third of our trucks stand still and we would still pay for them." FleetPath charges per route planned.

**Interview 7 (won, 200 trucks).** Won against FleetPath. "Per truck is easier to budget than per route." Pilot showed 6% fewer empty kilometres. Procurement negotiated hard; final discount 10%.

**Interview 8 (lost, 35 trucks).** Chose RouteOne. "For a small fleet like ours your minimum of 50 licences made you twice as expensive." Would have bought without the minimum.
