# Interview notes, case B

**Interview 9 (won, 90 trucks).** No competitor involved. Pilot showed 9% fewer empty kilometres. The buyer said the price was "fair for what we saw". Wanted quarterly billing; accepted annual.

**Interview 10 (lost, 110 trucks).** Chose RouteOne, which was "clearly cheaper". The buyer added that RouteOne offered a free pilot and we charged for ours.

**Interview 11 (lost, 250 trucks).** Seasonal business. "Paying per truck for trucks that are parked four months a year makes no sense." Did not buy from anyone.

**Interview 12 (won, 70 trucks).** Won against RouteOne despite a higher price. Reason: integration with their warehouse system was already available. Discount of 5% for annual prepayment.

**Interview 13 (lost, 40 trucks).** "The minimum of 50 licences killed it." Bought nothing; stays with spreadsheets.

**Interview 14 (lost, 500 trucks).** Chose FleetPath. "Your references stop at 200 trucks. We didn't believe the savings at our scale." Price was similar between both vendors.
